Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This policy describes how VEYLUNA Publisher, a locally operated tool controlled by the VEYLUNA YouTube channel owner, accesses and uses Google user data. This informational website does not request Google sign-in or collect account passwords.

Google data accessed

With the channel owner's authorization, VEYLUNA Publisher may access basic YouTube channel identity and channel information; information needed to upload and update the owner's videos, titles, descriptions, keywords, thumbnails, and playlists; and status information about uploads. It requests YouTube API permissions through Google OAuth.

How data is used

Google user data is used only to identify the channel designated by the account owner, upload generated content, manage video and playlist metadata, verify uploads, and prevent duplicate publishing. The application does not use YouTube account data for advertising profiling.

Storage and retention

OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in files on the computer running the automation and are used to authenticate future authorized YouTube operations. Generated videos and locally recorded upload manifests may be kept on that computer until manually deleted. This website itself does not store OAuth tokens or YouTube account data.

Sharing and disclosure

The locally operated publisher sends upload files and necessary metadata to Google's YouTube API to carry out the requested actions. It does not sell Google user data or intentionally share OAuth tokens with advertisers or data brokers. Google processes information according to its own policies when YouTube API services are used.

Security

The channel owner controls local access to the computer and OAuth credentials. Credentials should not be published in source-code repositories or shared with other people. There is no public account registration or login feature on this site.

Revoking access and deleting data

The account owner can revoke VEYLUNA Publisher's authorization at Google Account third-party connections. To delete locally retained application data, remove the local OAuth token, generated files, and upload manifests from the VEYLUNA installation. Existing videos on YouTube must be deleted separately in YouTube Studio if desired.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the tool's features or data practices change. The effective date shown above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this locally operated tool should be directed to the developer support email listed on its Google OAuth consent screen. Before production use, the publisher should replace this sentence with a dedicated support contact address.
