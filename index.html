VEYLUNA Publisher

Where Your Mind Finds Quiet

VEYLUNA Publisher is a personal desktop automation tool for preparing and uploading original relaxation music videos to a YouTube channel. The tool runs on a locally controlled computer and uses the YouTube Data API after the channel owner authorizes access through Google's OAuth flow.

What the app does

Generates original ambient music and visual assets, assembles long-form relaxation videos and short wellness videos, prepares descriptive metadata, and uploads those videos to the channel selected by its owner. The owner controls the authorization and publishing settings.

Why YouTube access is requested

The app needs permission to identify the authorized YouTube channel, upload video files, update video metadata and thumbnails, and manage related playlists. It does not ask visitors to sign in to this website. The publishing tool is intended for use by the channel owner, not the public.

Data and privacy

Read our privacy policy for details about Google data accessed, storage, retention, sharing, and how to revoke access.
